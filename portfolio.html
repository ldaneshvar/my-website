My Life

Short film · October 2026

Hike at Dusk, Lake 22

A short film from my hike to Lake 22 in Washington. I wanted to make an ordinary hiking video feel more cinematic and suspenseful, almost like a scene from a movie. I experimented with pacing, transitions, and sound to build tension while capturing the scenery and fading daylight. Edited in Adobe After Effects.

Short-form video · MTC · July 2026

Estuary Youth Council, July Short-Form Video

A short-form video documenting the Estuary Youth Council’s kickoff at India Basin Waterfront Park in San Francisco. The session brought together eight youth participants, community organizations, and environmental educators to discuss local environmental challenges, community-driven solutions, and the capstone projects participants would develop throughout the program.

Short-form video · MTC · August 2026

Estuary Youth Council, August Field Trip

A short-form video documenting the Estuary Youth Council’s field trip to the Silicon Valley Advanced Water Purification Center and Alviso Marina County Park. Participants learned about water purification and conservation, explored career opportunities in environmental management, and took a guided boat tour of Alviso Slough to learn about local wildlife, wetlands, and flood protection.
