Estuary Artist-in-Residence Campaign

Supported the launch of the San Francisco Estuary Partnership’s inaugural Artist-in-Residence program, centered on the 2026 theme Rising Seas. Developed five program webpages in WordPress, built the application form in Google Forms, and designed promotional materials in Canva and Figma. Also supported digital outreach across Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook through Hootsuite. The campaign connects art, environmental awareness, and community engagement while exploring the impacts of sea level rise on the San Francisco Estuary.
